# Style: Film

**One continuous take.** A 3D world fills the screen behind the page, and the camera travels through
it as you scroll. Sections pin and scrub like scenes in one shot, and the thread carries from the first
frame to the last. This is the style the contract describes by default, so this guide is short: where
it says nothing, follow the contract.

Sites in this style so far: Madalin's hot air balloon flight, Zsolt's gym, Ionuț's football pitch.

## Defaults for "auto" answers

- **Theme:** dark, or dark with one flash.
- **Palette:** one deep field and one saturated accent, as in the wizard's Graphite, Midnight or Ember.
- **Fonts:** a characterful display face with a plain body. Any preset suits; avoid the editorial serifs.
- **Look:** cinematic. **Motion:** balanced. **Length:** standard, about 25 screens.

## The page

- **Pinned first.** Most sections are pinned and scrubbed, with 1 screen of scrolling per beat. Use
  `flow` only for content taller than a screen, such as a long list of features.
- **The world is the page.** The canvas is always visible, with no opaque section backgrounds. Text
  sits over the 3D, so frame subjects away from it, as the contract describes.
- **Chrome:** a small pill of chapter links, top right, shown from 900px. The preloader is the name and
  a percentage.

## Structure

Use one of the two proven structures from `/site`, or a mix:

- **One object:** a hero object stays on screen and transforms across the page.
- **One journey:** the camera travels through a single landscape with no hard break.

## Sections

All the section skills apply as written. `section-work` and `section-faq` aren't offered in this
style. `section-process` works as a pinned story with numbered beats.

## Review
